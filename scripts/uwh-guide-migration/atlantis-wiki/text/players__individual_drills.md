# Individual Drills

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/players/individual_drills
- Path: `players/individual_drills`

¶ Description

These Individual Drills focus on reinforcing maneuvers or skills that players use often. While the drills can be done in a group the drills themselves focus on single player maneuvers and do not generally require more than 1 person to complete.
