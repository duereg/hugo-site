# Half-Time

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/game/protocols/half-time
- Path: `game/protocols/half-time`

¶ Description

Games are split into two halves with a short break between. Full length games consist of two fifteen minute halves and a three minute half time. At half time, teams will switch which end of the playing area they will start at. Referees will signal half time by forming a “T” with their forearms and signal the teams to switch sides. Half length and half time length may vary depending on what the tournament host allocates for the games.
