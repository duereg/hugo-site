¶ Description

There are several methods of [/club-organization/recruitment] recruiting new players that are helpful for brand new clubs and older clubs. 

Clubs can recruit online or by putting up posters or providing information packets at local pools and recreation centers. 

It is best to try to find people who are already comfortable swimming. One great place to recruit is among the lap swimmers at the pool. Many people would rather play a sport in the water rather than swimming laps alone. It can also work to recruit from swim teams, the swimmers may be interested in playing in their off season. Many players do not come form a swimming background though, so make sure to recruit in other areas as well.
