¶ Description

Often players can be recruited from the people who regularly go to the pool or recreation center for other purposes. It can be very helpful to provide information packets or posters that people can take home, or to put up banners in the pool area. Many people are excited by the idea that they can get a swim workout in without swimming laps!

Tings to include in info packets are:

 
Description of how the game works

 
Pictures of the game and the gear

 
How to contact the club

 
When the practices are
