¶ Disrupting Forward (FD)

← Return to Muffin Top

 [img:/underwater_hockey_pool_layout_variant.jpg alt:underwater_hockey_pool_layout_variant.jpg] 

The FD is the “Disrupting Forward” and can generally play anywhere in the pool except the 1m closest to their own goal, as shown in the grayed-out area of the image.

¶ Primary Purpose

Disrupt opponent structure, passing lanes, and offensive flow.

¶ Main Responsibilities

Interrupt opponent plays

Pressure passing lanes

Back-pick curls

Force mistakes and rushed decisions

Create chaos in transition situations

¶ Defensive Role

The FD focuses heavily on:

Disrupting the opposition’s teamwork

Breaking offensive rhythm

Applying pressure during transitions

This includes:

Entering passing lanes

Pressuring wall play

Poking pucks toward supporting teammates

¶ Offensive Role

When in possession, the FD should:

Quickly recycle the puck to:

Wings

Backs

Avoid holding possession too long

Return to the surface quickly to maintain awareness

¶ Positioning Guidelines

Can operate almost anywhere in the pool

Avoid the 1m area closest to the defended goal

¶ Surface Expectations

Often remains on the surface

Uses short, explosive dives strategically

Conserves energy for disruption opportunities

¶ Supporting Offensive Pushes

The FD may temporarily assist offensively when:

A strong offensive push develops

Additional support is needed in open water

This falls under the FD’s secondary responsibilities.

¶ Wall Play Behavior

¶ If the team is progressing successfully:

Remain on the surface

Monitor the developing play

¶ If the play stalls or becomes dangerous:

Drop ahead of the play

Pressure the opponent’s advance

Redirect the puck toward:

Wings

Backs

¶ If the FD gains possession near the wall:

Push toward the centerline

Recycle possession to a Back

Avoid becoming trapped against the wall
