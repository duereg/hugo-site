¶ Beginner Mistakes

Everyone makes beginner mistakes. The difference is whether you learn them the easy way (by reading this list) or the hard way (by repeating them in the pool).

1) Over-breathing or rushing before a dive

 
Taking frantic big breaths makes you feel worse, not better.

 
Fix: slow, relaxed breathing. Smooth inhale, calm exhale.

2) Staying down too long

 
Beginners often “push it” and surface stressed.

 
Fix: surface early. Build repeatable short dives first.

3) Losing the puck and chasing it wildly

 
Chasing creates collisions and wastes energy.

 
Fix: keep your body controlled, approach at an angle, use your stick to slow/stop the puck.

4) Forgetting to look up

 
New players stare at the puck and miss teammates, opponents, and walls.

 
Fix: “touch and scan” — brief look at puck, then look up.

5) Poor mask/snorkel setup

 
Leaks, fog, and snorkel discomfort ruin your session.

 
Fix: take time to fit gear properly; use defog; adjust strap tension (snug, not painful).

6) Using hands on the puck

 
It’s unsafe and usually against the rules.

 
Fix: puck is played with the stick only. Reset if you lose control.

7) Big fin splashes, not enough control

 
Big kicks wastes energy and doesnt result in faster swimming.

 
Fix: controlled finning, less splashing will be more effecient.
