# Social Events

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/club-organization/practices/social-events
- Path: `club-organization/practices/social-events`

¶ Description

Underwater hockey is about a lot more than playing the game and practicing skills. A club is also a close-knit community of life long friends. It is important for clubs to remember to organize social events outside of the pool to build up this community as much as possible. A great time to hang out as a team is for meals after practice. 

Getting to know teammates out of the water will improve in water teamwork significantly as well!
