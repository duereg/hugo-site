¶ Progressive Badges

Progressive badges measure player performance in underwater hockey beyond
the foundational level.

These badges contribute directly to player rank and reflect a player’s
ability to perform under increasing levels of demand.

Progression follows a Shallow → Mid → Deep → Pelagic system.
Each level represents increased control, consistency, and performance under pressure.

Progressive badges are introduced after completion of Foundation badges
and form the basis of rank progression beyond Coral.

¶ Conditioning Badges

¶ UW Swim

Shallow — 25m controlled underwater swim.

Mid — 50m controlled underwater swim.

Deep — 75m controlled underwater swim.

Pelagic — 100m controlled underwater swim.

¶ Static Breath Hold

Shallow — 60 seconds.

Mid — 120 seconds.

Deep — 180 seconds.

Pelagic — 240 seconds.

¶ 30s Simulations

Shallow — 5 chains.

Mid — 10 chains.

Deep — 15 chains.

Pelagic — 20 chains.

¶ Precision Badges

¶ Puck Batting

Shallow — Control rolling puck 3 times flicked by coach.

Mid — Control flicked puck 3 times.

Deep — Control contested puck 5 times.

Pelagic — Reliable puck control in game situations.

¶ Pass Accuracy

Shallow — 5 ground hits to 30cm target at 1m.

Mid — 4/5 flicks through 30cm elevated target.

Deep — 4/5 flicks through 20cm elevated target.

Pelagic — 4/5 flicks through puck-width opening.

¶ Pass Distance

Shallow — Controlled flick to target zone:
 
Male: 4ft | Female: 2ft

Mid — Controlled flick to target zone:
 
Male: 6ft | Female: 4ft

Deep — Controlled flick to target zone:
 
Male: 8ft | Female: 6ft

Pelagic — Controlled flick to target zone:
 
Male: 10ft | Female: 8ft

¶ Pass Height

Shallow — Clean flick over puck (3cm).

Mid — Flick over 15cm object.

Deep — Flick over 50cm marker.

Pelagic — Flick over 80cm–1m obstacle with control.

¶ Curl Mastery

Shallow — Retain possession after curl vs active defender (4/6).

Mid — Retain possession vs equal defender (4/6).

Deep — Curl leads to controlled continuation (4/6).

Pelagic — Curl creates offensive advantage (5/8).

¶ Power Badges

¶ 25m Flutter UW

Shallow — Under 18 seconds with correct propulsion.

Mid — Under 13 seconds.

Deep — Under 12 seconds.

Pelagic — Under 10 seconds with controlled recovery.

¶ 25m Dolphin UW

Shallow — Under 19 seconds with consistent body wave.

Mid — Under 14 seconds.

Deep — Under 13 seconds.

Pelagic — Under 12 seconds with streamlined posture.

¶ 50m Surface Sprint

Shallow — Under 45 seconds.

Mid — Under 40 seconds.

Deep — Under 35 seconds.

Pelagic — Under 30 seconds with efficiency.

¶ Explosive Start

Shallow — 12.5m in under 6.5 seconds.

Mid — Under 6.0 seconds.

Deep — Under 5.5 seconds.

Pelagic — Under 5.0 seconds.

¶ Coach Guidance

Progressive badges should be observed during normal training where possible.

Short testing blocks may be used for conditioning and power badges.

Do not interrupt game flow for technical testing.

Only sign off when performance is repeatable and controlled.

Prioritize game-relevant performance over isolated drill success.

Progressive badges reflect a player’s ability to perform under pressure
and are the primary drivers of rank progression beyond Coral.
