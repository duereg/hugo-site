# *Advertising the Tournament

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/club-organization/hosting-tournaments/advertising-the-tournament
- Path: `club-organization/hosting-tournaments/advertising-the-tournament`

¶ Description

Spreading the word about a tournament is important for making sure enough teams come to make it a competitive tournament. Here are a few methods for spreading the word about your tournament.

 
Have the tournament listed on the UWHPortal.

 
Ask the local federation to send out an announcement.

 
Post about it on social media

 
Reach out to close club leaders.
