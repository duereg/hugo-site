¶ Level 2: Foundations of Hockey

Who this is for: Players who have completed Level 1 or can safely participate in Underwater Hockey and understand the basic objective of the game.

Goal of this level: Develop the technical skills, movement patterns, decision-making, and game understanding required to become a reliable and effective hockey player.

Typical delivery: Five developmental blocks delivered over multiple sessions with repeated practice and game application.

By the end of Level 2, players should be able to:

 
Move efficiently into strong playing positions

 
Control and protect the puck under pressure

 
Pass and receive consistently

 
Apply basic attacking and defensive principles

 
Support teammates effectively

 
Understand positions, formations, and the flow of the game

 
Participate confidently in structured team play

Development tracking: Foundation Badge skills continue to be introduced and observed throughout Level 2. Coaches should use normal training and game situations to assess progress without interrupting the learning experience.

¶ Level 2 Learning Blocks

 [/en/Coaching/Level-2/Block-1] Block 1 – Strong Position & Movement

Develop efficient movement patterns, strong body position, effective diving techniques, and surface recovery to arrive ready to play.

 [/en/Coaching/Level-2/Block-2] Block 2 – Puck Control

Build reliable puck control through passing, receiving, swimming with the puck, and protecting possession under pressure.

 [/en/Coaching/Level-2/Block-3] Block 3 – Attack & Defence Principles

Learn the fundamental principles of attacking and defending while developing strong individual positioning and decision-making.

 [/en/Coaching/Level-2/Block-4] Block 4 – Support & Decision Making

Develop support play, dive timing, rotational movement, and teamwork to become an effective teammate.

 [/en/Coaching/Level-2/Block-5] Block 5 – Game Understanding & Rules

Combine technical skills and game principles within structured match play while learning positions, formations, and rules.

¶ Level 2 Philosophy

 
Position before possession.

 
Control before speed.

 
Decision-making before tactics.

 
Support before individual play.

 
Consistency before complexity.

Level 2 develops players who no longer simply react to the game but begin making purposeful decisions based on sound hockey principles.

A successful Level 2 player is not yet an advanced player. A successful Level 2 player moves efficiently, controls and protects the puck with confidence, supports teammates, understands the principles of attack and defence, and contributes effectively within a structured team environment.
