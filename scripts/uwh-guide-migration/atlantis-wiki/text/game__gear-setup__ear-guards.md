# Ear-Guards

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/game/gear-setup/ear-guards
- Path: `game/gear-setup/ear-guards`

¶ Description

Players must have sturdy ear protection when playing. Without headgear it is possible to get hit in the ear, causing high water pressure in the inner ear that can result in damage. Most commonly players wear water polo caps, but other forms of ear protection, such as wrestling head gear, are used as well.
