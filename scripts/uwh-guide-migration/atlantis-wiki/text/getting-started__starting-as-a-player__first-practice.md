# *First Practice

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/getting-started/starting-as-a-player/first-practice
- Path: `getting-started/starting-as-a-player/first-practice`

¶ Description

Many new players have no idea what to expect at their first underwater hockey practice. This is ok, everyone starts that way! Here are a few things to do to make it easier to join:

 
Look up a few videos of underwater hockey games! This will help you understand better how a game runs and make everything a bit more familiar when you join in.

 
Make sure to contact someone at the club ahead of time so that someone can arrive early and help out with finding the gear and give an intro.

 
When playing for the first game, focus on getting down to receive passes, even if it is not clear the pass will come to you.

 
Ask the club members any questions.
