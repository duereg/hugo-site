# Individual Fundamentals

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/players/individual_fundamentals
- Path: `players/individual_fundamentals`

¶ Description

Individual Fundamentals for Underwater Hockey are aspects of play that many other maneuvers are based on or require to perform. They include best practices for how players should 
 [/players/individual_fundamentals/moving_in_the_water] Moving through the Water, including body positioning at the playing surface, as well as the building blocks that 
 [/players/individual_fundamentals/puck_handling] Puck Handling maneuvers are made from.
