# *Housing

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/club-organization/hosting-tournaments/housing
- Path: `club-organization/hosting-tournaments/housing`

¶ Description

Tournament organizers are not expected to organize housing for teams, but it can be helpful to suggest a few hotels that are close to the pool and reasonably priced. It may also be helpful to talk to nearby hotels and ask them to hold blocks of rooms for hockey players to use. 

For smaller tournaments, clubs sometimes also offer some local housing with club members, allowing players a free place to stay. This could make attending the tournament possible for a few more players.
