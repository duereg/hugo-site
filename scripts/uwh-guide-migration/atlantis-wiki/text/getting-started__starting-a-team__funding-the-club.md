# *Funding the Club

- Path: `getting-started/starting-a-team/funding-the-club`

¶ Description

The main expense that a club has is pool costs. These are most easily covered by charging players a fee for each practice. It can be helpful to make a business checking account for the club and collect fees form players and pay the pool from that account.  

Once a club has been running for a while and has several consistent players, it may be worthwhile to allow new players a few free practices. Making a player's first three practices free, for example, may encourage more people to come try out the sport. This is not always possible and the ability of a club to do this will depend on how many consistent players they have and how high pool expenses are. 

Another excellent way for a club to make some money is to [/club-organization/hosting-tournaments] host a tournament. This takes a lot of work, and many people are needed to do all this, but hosting a tournament is lots of fun and can help build up a club.
