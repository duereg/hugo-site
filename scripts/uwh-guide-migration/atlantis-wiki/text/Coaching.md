¶ Atlantis Coaching Programme

Welcome to the Atlantis Coaching Programme.
This pathway provides a structured progression from a player's first experience in the water through to advanced team play and competitive Underwater Hockey.
Each level builds on the previous one, developing confident, skilled, and intelligent players.

¶ Level 1 — Learn the Game

Introduce new players to Underwater Hockey by developing confidence, safety, and enjoyment in the aquatic environment.

¶ Players develop:

 
Water confidence and safe participation.

 
Equipment skills and basic movement.

 
First puck control and simple hockey skills.

 
An understanding of the rules and objective of the game.

¶ Level 2 — Fundamental Skills

Develop the technical skills and core principles required to play Underwater Hockey confidently.

¶ Players develop:

 
Efficient movement and body position.

 
Puck control, passing, receiving, and protection.

 
Basic attacking and defensive principles.

 
Support, decision-making, and game understanding.

¶ Level 3 — Advanced Skills & Team Application

Expand the player's technical toolbox while introducing how advanced individual skills are applied within team play.

¶ Players develop:

 
Advanced attacking and defensive techniques.

 
Advanced passing, receiving, and puck control skills.

 
Movement without the puck and support play.

 
Applying individual skills within small group and team situations.

¶ Level 4 — Advanced Collective Play

Combine advanced individual skills into coordinated team systems and tactical play.

¶ Players develop:

 
Collective skills and multi-player combinations.

 
Advanced rotations and team structure.

 
Formation understanding and tactical systems.

 
Game intelligence, tactical adaptation, and competitive performance.

¶ Coaching Philosophy

Every level follows the same learning pathway:

Learn the Skill
 ↓
Understand When to Use It
 ↓
Apply It with Teammates
 ↓
Use It in Game Play

This progression ensures players develop strong individual skills before learning how to combine those skills into effective team play.
