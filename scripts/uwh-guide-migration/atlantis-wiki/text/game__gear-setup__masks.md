# Masks

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/game/gear-setup/masks
- Path: `game/gear-setup/masks`

¶ Description

Masks are worn by all players and must have shatter resistant lenses.  Masks must have a dual lens design, or have a center divider providing support or be a plastic monolithic frame design mask.

Masks are preferred over goggles as it keep water out of the players nose and makes it easier to control the pressure in the mask by releasing air through the nose into the mask.
