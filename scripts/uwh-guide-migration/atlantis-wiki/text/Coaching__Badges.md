¶ Underwater Hockey Badge System

The Atlantis Underwater Hockey Badge System is used to document player development
across the core areas of the sport.

Badges are awarded through coaching observation during normal training.
They are designed to support coaching, not replace it or turn sessions into formal testing.

¶ How Coaches Use This System

 
Run the coaching session as normal.

 
Observe whether a player demonstrates a skill consistently.

 
Sign off the relevant badge level when the performance is clearly repeatable.

 
Record progress in the player tracker.

Badges should document development without interrupting session flow.
If a player is not clearly ready, leave the badge unsigned and reassess later.

¶ Badge Categories

 
 [/en/Coaching/Badges/Foundation] Foundation — Basic competency and safety.

 
 [/en/Coaching/Badges/Progressive] Conditioning — Breath control and endurance.

 
 [/en/Coaching/Badges/Progressive] Precision — Puck control and passing accuracy.

 
 [/en/Coaching/Badges/Progressive] Power — Propulsion and speed.

 
 [/en/Coaching/Badges/Skills] Tactical Skills — Advanced puck manipulation techniques.

 
 [/en/Coaching/Badges/Leadership] Leadership — Coaching contribution.

 
 [/en/Coaching/Badges/Participation-Milestone] Participation — Commitment to the sport.

 
 [/en/Coaching/Badges/Achievement] Achievement — Competition milestones.

¶ Rank Structure

The ranking system follows an ocean depth progression model:

 
Coral — Foundation complete

 
Reef — Early developing player

 
Ocean Current — Reliable club player

 
Deep Blue — Competitive player

 
Pelagic — High performance level

Before entering the ranking system, players must complete all
 [/en/Coaching/Badges/Foundation] Foundation badges.
This represents the minimum competency required to play underwater hockey safely and effectively.

After Coral rank is achieved, progression is determined through badge points in
 [/en/Coaching/Badges/Progressive] Conditioning,
 [/en/Coaching/Badges/Progressive] Precision, and
 [/en/Coaching/Badges/Progressive] Power.

¶ Coach Guidance

 
Coach the session first.

 
Use badges to document what is already being demonstrated.

 
Do not stop sessions to run formal badge tests unless specifically required.

 
Sign off only when performance is calm, safe, and repeatable.

Badges document development. They do not replace coaching.
