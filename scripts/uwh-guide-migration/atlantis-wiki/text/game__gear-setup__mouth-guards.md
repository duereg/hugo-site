# Mouth-Guards

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/game/gear-setup/mouth-guards
- Path: `game/gear-setup/mouth-guards`

¶ Description

Mouth guards are firm plastic pieces that attach to the snorkel over the players mouth. These protect players from injuring their teeth if they are hit in the mouth by another player or the puck.
