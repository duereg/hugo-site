# Tackling Maneuvers

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/players/tackling_maneuvers
- Path: `players/tackling_maneuvers`

¶ Description

Tackling maneuvers are options available to a player when an opponent has possession of the [/game/gear-setup/pucks] puck. They can be used to take or “steal” the puck from an opponent. 

There are situations where just hitting or “bashing” the puck off the stick towards a teammate is appropriate, but in general it is better to steal the puck with a tackling maneuver in order to establish control of the puck and then think about making progress or [/players/individual_fundamentals/puck_handling/passes] passing to a teammate.
