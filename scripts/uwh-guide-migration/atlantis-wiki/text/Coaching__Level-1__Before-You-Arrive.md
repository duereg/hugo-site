¶ Before You Arrive

¶ Purpose

A player's first experience often determines whether they return.

Preparing players before they arrive reduces anxiety, improves safety, saves valuable pool time, and allows coaches to focus on introducing Underwater Hockey rather than solving equipment problems.

This page provides a simple checklist for coaches to complete before a player's first session.

¶ New Player Questionnaire

Collect the following information before the player's first session.

¶ Player Information

Full Name

Email Address

Phone Number

Emergency Contact Name

Emergency Contact Phone Number

¶ Swimming Experience

Ask about the player's previous experience.

Can you swim confidently?

Have you used a snorkel before?

Have you worn fins before?

Have you tried freediving or scuba diving?

Have you played Underwater Hockey before?

¶ Coach Focus

Understanding a player's water confidence helps coaches provide the right level of support during their first session.

¶ Equipment

Record:

Shoe Size

Equipment already owned:

Mask

Snorkel

Mouthguard

Fins

Glove

Stick

Cap

¶ Coach Focus

Knowing what equipment players already have allows coaches to prepare any loan equipment before the session begins.

¶ Attendance

Encourage new players to commit to attending at least two introductory sessions within their first two weeks.

¶ Coach Focus

Most players require more than one session before they become comfortable with the equipment and the game.

¶ Welcome Information

Send new players a welcome message before their first session.

Include:

Pool location

Arrival time (30 minutes before the session)

Session duration

Parking information (if required)

Coach contact details

Link to the introductory video

¶ What to Bring

Ask players to bring:

Swimsuit

Towel

Water bottle

Socks (recommended for fin comfort)

If players own equipment, ask them to bring:

Mask

Snorkel

Fins

Mouthguard

¶ Coach Preparation Checklist

Before the player arrives, ensure:

☐ Loan equipment is available in the correct sizes.

☐ Beginner stick tape is ready for player identification.

☐ Spare masks and snorkels are available.

☐ Spare mouthguards are available if required.

☐ Session plan is prepared.

☐ Experienced players understand they will be supporting beginners.
