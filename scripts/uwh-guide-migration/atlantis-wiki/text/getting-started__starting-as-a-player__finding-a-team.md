# *Finding a Team

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/getting-started/starting-as-a-player/finding-a-team
- Path: `getting-started/starting-as-a-player/finding-a-team`

¶ Description

Finding a team to practice with can be done in a couple of ways:

 
First, players can reach out to the country's federation and ask if there are any clubs in the area, and find a contact form the club.

 
Players can look at UWH tourist, a database with many clubs all over the world, including contacts for the clubs. This can be found at pucku.org/tourist/.
