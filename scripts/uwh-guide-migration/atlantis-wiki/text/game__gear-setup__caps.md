# Caps

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/game/gear-setup/caps
- Path: `game/gear-setup/caps`

¶ Description

Players must play with caps corresponding to their stick color during games. Caps should also show the player’s number. The cap color makes it easier for players to identify teammates and opponents. Referees also use the cap color and number to identify players that committed a foul or scored a goal.  Most commonly players use water polo caps.
