¶ What is Underwater Hockey?

Underwater Hockey is a team sport played on the bottom of a swimming pool. Two teams compete to move a puck along the pool floor
and score by pushing it into the opposing goal using a short stick.

Basic idea:

 
Play happens mostly underwater on the pool bottom.

 
Players take short breath-hold “dives” to play, then return to the surface to breathe.

 
The puck is played with the stick only (no hands).

 
It’s fast, fun, and very teamwork-focused.

What you’ll notice right away:

 
Everyone rotates between underwater effort and surface recovery.

 
Good positioning matters more than raw speed.

 
Control and calm breathing are your superpowers as a beginner.
