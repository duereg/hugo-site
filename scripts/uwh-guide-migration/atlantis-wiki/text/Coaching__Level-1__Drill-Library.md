¶ Drill Library

This library contains drills and activities that support Level 1 coaching.
Drills are grouped by session and aligned with the skills introduced during a player's first three sessions of Underwater Hockey.

¶ Session Drill Libraries

 [/en/Coaching/Level-1/Drill-Library/Session-1] Session 1 – Play Your First Game

Equipment confidence, first puck contact, basic puck movement, passing, and mini games.

 [/en/Coaching/Level-1/Drill-Library/Session-2] Session 2 – Control the Puck

Puck control, turning, passing while moving, support play, and possession games.

 [/en/Coaching/Level-1/Drill-Library/Session-3] Session 3 – Attack & Defend

Attacking play, defending play, transition, team roles, and game understanding.
