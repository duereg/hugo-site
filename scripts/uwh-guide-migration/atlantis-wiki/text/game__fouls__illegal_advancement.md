# Illegal Advancement of the Puck

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/game/fouls/illegal_advancement
- Path: `game/fouls/illegal_advancement`

¶ Description

Illegal advancement of the [/game/gear-setup/pucks] puck is called if the puck is moved with anything other than the legal playing section of the [/game/gear-setup/sticks] stick. The most frequent occurrence of this foul is advancing the puck with the [/game/gear-setup/gloves] glove (known as "gloving"), but includes any advancement of the puck using the free hand or any other part of the body. Incidental contact between the puck and glove will not be called as a foul unless the contact is deliberately used to advance the puck.

¶ When and How to Call

This should be called if a player gains an advantage by advancing the puck with anything other than their stick. The signal to call this foul is done by punching one fist into the palm of the other hand.

 [img:/illegal_advancement.png alt:] 

¶ When not to Call and What to Watch Out For

Illegal advancement should not be called if there is incidental contact between the puck and the glove that does not give the player any significant advantage. This is true for incidental contact between any body part or piece of gear.
