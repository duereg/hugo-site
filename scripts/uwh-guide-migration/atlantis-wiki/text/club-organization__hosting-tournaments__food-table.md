- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/club-organization/hosting-tournaments/food-table
- Path: `club-organization/hosting-tournaments/food-table`

¶ Description

Providing food for tournament participants to eat between games is an optional addition to the tournament, but is often greatly appreciated. Since there are often four to six games a day for teams, it is hard to go out and eat a full meal during the day. Because of this, players appreciate having food at the pool that they can snack on throughout the day. A little extra can be added on to the team entry fee to cover the cost of the food. 

It is up to the organizers to decide what food and how much food to provide, but here is a general idea of what can be provided:

 
Bread and sandwich toppings (PB&J, meats, cheeses, hummus, etc.)

 
Cup noodles

 
Bananas

 
Fruits and veggies 

 
Chips and dips

 
Sweets (cookies, candy)

 
Gatorade
