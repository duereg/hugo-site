# Evasive Maneuvers

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/players/evasive_maneuvers
- Path: `players/evasive_maneuvers`

¶ Description

Evasive Maneuvers are options available to a player when they have possession of the puck. They can be used to make progress around an opponent, protect the puck from an opponent, or both simultaneously. 

Not every maneuver is appropriate for any given situation, but a player having many (or all) of these maneuvers at the ready provides the most flexibility to be successful in whatever situation they may find themselves in during play.
