¶ Participation and Milestone Badges

Participation and milestone badges recognize long-term involvement,
commitment, and contribution to the sport.

These badges do not influence player rank. They exist to acknowledge
consistency, development, and engagement over time.

¶ Badge Criteria

¶ Consistent Player

Bronze — 75% attendance over 3 months.

Silver — 75% attendance over 6 months.

Gold — 75% attendance over 12 months.

¶ Practice Commitment

Bronze — Active membership for 1 year.

Silver — Active membership for 3 years.

Gold — Active membership for 5 years.

¶ Tournament Milestones

Level 1 — First tournament completed.

Level 2 — Multiple tournaments completed.

Level 3 — Consistent tournament participation across a season.

¶ Coach Guidance

Participation badges should be tracked over time.

Use simple attendance or participation records.

Award badges as soon as criteria is met.

These badges support retention and long-term engagement.

Participation and milestone badges recognize commitment and consistency,
which are essential for long-term player development.
