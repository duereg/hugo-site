¶ Leadership Badges

Leadership badges recognize coaching contribution, session leadership,
and progression from assisting within a club environment to leading
structured programs and teams.

These badges do not influence player rank. They exist to acknowledge
leadership, responsibility, and contribution to the development of others.

Leadership badges are awarded through demonstrated contribution over time,
not through isolated session performance.

¶ Badge Criteria

¶ Coaching Assistant

Level 1 — Assisted in 5 structured training sessions.

Level 2 — Assisted in 15 sessions and led warm-up independently.

Level 3 — Planned and delivered 3 structured sessions.

Level 4 — Mentored a new assistant coach for a minimum of 1 season.

¶ Coaching

Level 1 — Active club coach for 1 season.

Level 2 — Delivered a structured seasonal program.

Level 3 — Led a team at an official tournament.

Level 4 — Regional or national coaching appointment.

¶ Coach Guidance

Leadership badges should be awarded based on verified contribution over time.

These badges recognize responsibility, planning, delivery, and mentorship.

Do not award leadership badges for one-off help unless the stated criteria are met.

Use simple records of session assistance, delivery, and coaching roles.

Leadership badges recognize contribution to the growth of the sport and the
development of other players and coaches.
