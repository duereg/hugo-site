¶ Atlantis Sports – Underwater Hockey Wiki

Underwater Hockey (UWH) is a fast-paced, team sport played on the bottom of a pool; combining skill, strategy, and teamwork. During play players make repeated short dives to support their teammates while pushing, pulling, and passing the puck using short one-handed sticks in order to score in the goal at each end.

This wiki is designed for players, coaches, and club organizers looking to deepen their understanding of underwater hockey. If you are a pool manager looking for information about the sport to see if it is safe for your patrons and facility, please see our [/pool-managers] facility or aquatics manager page.

This resource also serves as the foundation for developing set of structured coaching programs and a player badge system, designed to guide skill progression, track development, and support consistent coaching across all levels of play.

¶ What is Underwater Hockey?

Underwater hockey is played in a pool typically two to three meters (six to nine feet) deep. The [/game/gear-setup/pucks] puck, similar in size to an ice hockey puck, weighs about 1.3kg (~2.8 lbs) so it stays on the bottom.

Players wear a mask, fins, and snorkel, and must dive down to the pool floor to advance the puck. They use short, one-handed [/game/gear-setup/sticks] sticks, which are the only legal way to move the puck.

The [/game/gear-setup/goals] goal is a three-meter-long trough positioned on the bottom of the pool at each end of the playing area.
