# *Types of Tournaments

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/club-organization/hosting-tournaments/types-of-tournaments
- Path: `club-organization/hosting-tournaments/types-of-tournaments`

¶ Description

There are a few types of tournaments.

 
Club Tournaments
 
These tournaments are made up of club teams, meaning each team is made up of only players that regularly practice with the team.

 
 

 
Team Tournaments
 
These tournaments are made up of teams that can be put together form any players, there are no restrictions about which teams players can play on. For example, a group of women could come together form several clubs to make an all women's team, or lefty's from all over could combine to make a lefty team.

 
 

 
Potluck Tournaments
 
This kind of tournament is when players sign up individually and the organizers then put together mixed teams, all of roughly the same level. This format is excellent for players who can't get a full team committed to going to the tournament. This is also a great way for players to share skills and tips between clubs and grow individual skills.

 
 

¶ Examples

Examples of each of these types of tournaments in the US include Nationals, which is a club only tournament, Battle at Altitude, which has no restrictions on teams, and the University of Illinois Trench Warfare potluck tournament.
