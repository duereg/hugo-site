# *Online Recruitment

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/club-organization/recruitment/online-recruitment
- Path: `club-organization/recruitment/online-recruitment`

¶ Description

Many people seek out new clubs and activities online, and club development can greatly benefit from a strong online presence. Social media presence can raise awareness of the sport, and can catch the attention of several potential recruits. 

It is also helpful to make a club website where people that are interested can go to learn more about the sport, where the club practices, and how to get in touch with a club representative.
