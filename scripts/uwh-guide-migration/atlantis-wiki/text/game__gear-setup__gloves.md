# Gloves

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/game/gear-setup/gloves
- Path: `game/gear-setup/gloves`

¶ Description

Players will have hand protection over the hand that is holding the stick. This consists of a glove that covers the hand with a soft padding. The glove protects the player's hand from getting scraped up on the bottom of the pool and protects from incidental contact with the puck. The glove must be a color that contrasts the stick color and the puck color and it may not have any rigid or sharp material for reinforcement.
