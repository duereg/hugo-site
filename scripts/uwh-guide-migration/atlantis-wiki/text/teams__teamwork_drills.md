# Teamwork Drills

- URL: https://wiki.atlantissports.org/teams/teamwork_drills
- Path: `teams/teamwork_drills`

¶ Description

Teamwork drills are an excellent way to practice as a team in the water. Since players can't communicate well underwater during the game, it is even more important for players to determine their strategies before playing. Practicing this with drills is an excellent way to get everyone on the same page.
