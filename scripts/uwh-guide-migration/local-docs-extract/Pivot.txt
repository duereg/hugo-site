Pivot
------------------------
 - Your job is the control the middle of the formation and the play
 - You must always be ready to support the wing forwards
 - You must always be ready to be a forward option of the wing backs and swing

When you have the puck
----------------------
 - Your first thought should be pass the puck to a teammate in a better position to score.
 - If you cannot pass, look to score. 
 - Do not curl
 - You can swim straight ahead or towards either side of the formation
 - Do not swim outside of the wing forwards
 - All of your help is around you.
 - If you lose the puck, lose it in the center of the formation
 - If you are on the wall look to advance the puck to a forward and get off the wall.
 - If you reach the corner, move down the end wall.
  * keep swimming

 - You can make small moves towards either side of the formation
  * You cannot cross the formation (ie control the middle)
 - You can pass to either the wing forwards or the wing backs
 - You cannot pass to the swing
  * This should not be a long pass
  * If you need to throw more than 6 feet, don't make the pass
 - You cannot leave the puck. Make a pass to a teammate

When you no longer have the puck
--------------------------------
 - You should surface in the direction the play is going
  - If you pass to the wing forwards, surface forward and chase the play
 - If you pass to a wing back, surface in the direction that the play is going.
  - If you leave the puck for the wing back on your side, surface forward in front of that back
  - If you lose the puck backwards, surface backwards to stay close to the puck

When the backs on your team have the puck behind you
----------------------------------------------------------------
 - You should be either forward of the back within passing distance or shoulder to shoulder with the back helping to drive the play
 - You should be in the middle of the formation



When a back on your team has the puck in front of you
----------------------------------------------------------------
 - You need to get shoulder to shoulder with him or get in front of him.
  

When the swing has the puck
---------------------------
 - You should be ready to receive a pass from the swing
