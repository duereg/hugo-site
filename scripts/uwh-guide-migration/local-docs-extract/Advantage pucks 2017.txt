Advantage Puck Setup The goal is to get the puck to the pivot who is facing backwards (towards the puck). We will set one of the backwards forward as a “blocker”. His job is to make sure that if we don’t score, the puck is cleared to our right corner. The swing should shoot and chase the puck up into the scrum.  1

2 If the other team sets up anything close to this the play is to the open side. Advantage Puck Setup The whole team is setup behind the line.  The wings drop facing our goal, the pivot drops on the puck facing our backs The goal of this play is to go to the openside half back. If it’s not there the swing will use a hand signal to call it off and the pivot slides it to a charging inside back going up the wall. 3
