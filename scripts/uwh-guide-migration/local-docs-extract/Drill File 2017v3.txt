2 on 1 Open side passing drill Forward starts with puck swimming open As the defender attacks, slide it to the back supporting. Forward continues swimming towards open pool. Back picks up the puck and continues swimming open pool.

--- slide ---
2 on 1 Open side passing drill Back now has the puck and continues swimming while drawing the defender in. Forward lifts to mid pool so as not to screen allowing the defender to go under him. When the opportunity is there the back sends the puck to the forward and keeps swimming. As the forward receives the puck he drops down and seals the defender off and swims.

3 on 2 Wall Passing Goals Drill is shown on the left wall. We will drill both left and right wall. USA Players To make a clean pass either along the wall or to the pivot To develop the skill to receive the puck while facing forward (assumption is we are on the attack) and backward (assumption is our back just made a defensive stop and the forward was coming back to help). To make the next phase pass from either the pivot to the wing or vice verse. Other Players To make a clean tackle and gain control of the puck stopping a three on two and swim away with the puck. To take the puck using other methods than simply slapping the puck back towards the offensive player To develop proper technique as the second defender into the play to stop the attack.

3 on 2 wall passing drill v1 Working the wall channel passing to teammate while facing opponent. Hit either the Wing forward or the pivot. The wing and pivot should be able to look to either side to see the puck Pass to the best option and support the receiver.

--- slide ---
3 on 2 wall passing drill v1 If the wing gets the puck, they should make progress. Once stopped by the defender, center the puck, in the formation, to the pivot. Pivot should swim till stopped. The half back can surface after the pass to the wing but must be back as quick as possible to support the wing or pivot.

--- slide ---
3 on 2 wall passing drill v1 If the pivot gets the puck, they should make progress. Before being stopped but after attracting the defender, they should send the puck to the wing. Wing should swim till they are stopped. The half back can surface if needed but must be down as quick as possible to support his wing.

--- slide ---
3 on 2 wall passing drill v2 Working the wall channel to teammate while facing opponent. Hit either the Wing forward or the pivot. Pass to the best option and support the receiver.

--- slide ---
3 on 2 wall passing drill v2 If the wing gets the puck, they should turn around and make progress. Once stopped by the defender, center the puck, in the formation, to the pivot. Pivot should swim till stopped. The half back can surface after the pass to the wing but must be back as quick as possible to support the wing or pivot.

--- slide ---
3 on 2 wall passing drill v2 If the pivot gets the puck facing backwards he can either turn around or without turning around send the puck to the inside forward. If the pivot quick passes he must turn around and support. If he turns before passing he must make progress before passing. Wing should swim till they are stopped. The half back can surface if needed but must be down as quick as possible to support his wing.

3 on 2 Openside Drill Goals Drill is shown going right to left. We will also drill it going left to right. USA Players To build on the 2 on 1 Openside Drill by adding the pivot. To make safest pass to either the openside wing or the pivot. If it goes to the pivot to be able to make a safe second phase pass to the openside forward and continue the attack Other Players Develop the ability to make a clean tackle and take control of the puck without slapping the puck behind the offender. To Develop the ability to swim away from the opposing team while maintain control of the puck. To develop proper positioning as the second defender to stop a teams second phase.

3 on 2 Open side passing drill Forward starts with puck swimming open As the defender attacks, slide it to the back supporting. The pivot surfaces and then returns Forward continues swimming towards open pool. Back picks up the puck and continues swimming towards open pool.

--- slide ---
3 on 2 Open side passing drill Back now has the puck and continues swimming while drawing the defender in. If a second defender has the wing covered the half back can center the puck, in the formation, to the pivot. If the pivot receives the puck, they need to draw the defender in and go vertical.

--- slide ---
3 on 2 Open side passing drill If the pivot gets the puck he should go vertical and draw in the second defender. The wing should adjust and go vertical not open. The pivot should send the puck to the wing forward vertically and chase the play. If the pass option is not there pivot should continue swimming with the puck. The wing back should support the vertical attack.

To develop a quality pass from the inside wing to the pivot in a way that allows the pivot to make a quick pass to the openside To develop a quality pass from the pivot to the openside wing allowing the wing to swim away from the defense and start the attack

USA Players To make clean passes along the wall channel To use either side of the defender to complete a pass to your teammate To develop the skill to receive the puck while facing forward (assumption is we are on the attack) and backward (assumption is our back just made a defensive stop and the forward was coming back to help). 2 on 1 wall passing drill Back facing the puck v1 Working the wall channel passing to teammate while facing opponent. Swim at the defender. If there is space along the wall, send the puck up the wall and chase. Forward needs to be in the fins of the defender facing his own goal able to look to either side to see the puck. In position that allows the puck to come through or being able to reach back for the puck.

--- slide ---
2 on 1 wall passing drill Back facing the puck v2 Working the wall channel passing to teammate while facing opponent. Swim at the defender. If there is space along the wall, send the puck up the wall and chase. Forward needs to be in the fins of the defender facing backwards. In position to gain control of the puck and turn around and head up pool.

--- slide ---
2 on 1 wall passing drill Back facing the puck v1 Working the wall channel passing to teammate while facing opponent. If the defender closes off the wall, swim off the wall a little to create space and then send it to the forward and chase the puck. Forward needs to be in the fins of the defender facing his own goal able to look to either side to see the puck. In position that allows the puck to come through or being able to reach back for the puck.

2 on 1 wall passing drill Back facing the puck v2 Working the wall channel passing to teammate while facing opponent. If the defender closes off the wall, swim off the wall a little to create space and then send it to the forward and chase the puck. Forward needs to be in the fins of the defender facing backwards. In position to gain control of the puck and turn around and head up pool.

--- slide ---
2 on 1 wall passing drill Back facing away Working the wall channel passing to teammate while facing opponent. Swim at the defender to draw him in. Pass to either side of the stick and chase the puck. The forward needs to be basically on top of the defender so he can block him off from the puck as he receives the puck without making contact before he receives the puck.

For forwards to develop the ability to continue swimming with the play while not causing a screen and present a target for the back to pass to. For backs to be able to receive a slider from a forward while moving and be able to continue swimming to open pool To make a clean and safe pass to the forward and support the attack.
