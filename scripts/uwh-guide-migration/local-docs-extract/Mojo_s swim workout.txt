Mojo’s swim workout

Warm Up
300 swim
200 kick with sculling drills
200 BOGDAT

6 x 50 swim (take :10 rest between each swim)
descend 1-3 and 4-6 which means make #2 faster than #1 and # faster than #2 and repeat for 4-6

8 x 100 (take :20-:30 rest between each swim)
Odd 100s do the first 25 sprint underwater kick, remaining 75 should be strong swim.
Even 100s do the first 50 as ½ under (pretend you are striking the puck) and half on the surface and the remaining 50 as a smooth swim

6 x 50 kick on 1:00 + 100 swim (each 100 should be faster than the one before)

100 swim down

2800 yards or meters
