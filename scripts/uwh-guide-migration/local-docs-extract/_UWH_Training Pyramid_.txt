 
1)                    The Underwater Hockey Training Pyramid
a.   What is it?
i. Motivational model for managing training and personal development
ii.                     A guideline for physical training and mental understanding of the sport
b.  Pyramid Levels
i. Fitness
ii.                     Individual Skills
iii.                   Awareness
iv.                  Team Work
v.                   Strategies
vi.                  Psyche
c.   Pyramid Building 
i. Each level of the pyramid
1.  Represents a different phase of training
2.  Builds on the previous level
ii.                     If a level is skipped or not fully achieved, the next level cannot be built on successfully
iii.                   Start off at the base and work to the top level
 
2)   Fitness
	•	The base the Pyramid
	•	The Pyramid’s Foundation
	•	Higher Fitness ➔ Larger pyramid base to build other levels upon
	•	Fitness = Conditioning + Strength + Endurance + Stamina
	•	Conditioning is a regimen of
	•	Lots of swimming workouts with and/or without fins
	•	Underwater Hockey practices
	•	Proper Nutrition
	•	Balanced Diet
	•	Vitamins & Supplements
	•	Sufficient rest for recovery
	•	Strength
	•	Achieve through weight training
	•	High reps with low to medium weights
	•	Endurance (Aerobic Base Training)
	•	Low to medium intensity long distance swimming with or  without fins, on the surface and underwater
	•	Stamina (Anaerobic Threshold Training)
	•	High intensity short to medium distance swimming with  fins on the surface and underwater
	•	Swimming Combinations
	•	HUHO
	•	HOHU
	•	BOGDAT
	•	OUOU
	•	UOUO
	•	UUUU
	•	TOFU
	•	Reminder:
	•	Make sure to get a good warm-up and a  decent warm-down  after exercising
3)   Individual Skills
	•	Diving
	•	Head First
	•	Always rotate and glide behind the play to the puck
	•	Like an airplane landing, with the puck elbow bent
	•	Will provides maximum leverage to the puck
	•	Vertical head dive to the puck
	•	Arm is vertical to the puck
	•	Has little or no leverage when contacting the puck
	•	Feet First
	•	The prefer dive for equal puck situation
	•	Provide better vision of the surrounding environment
	•	Good for blocking opposition who is behind you
	•	Especially during  defensive corner play
	•	Inverted/Upside Down
	•	Good for blind-siding the opposition
	•	Use the opposition blind spot to contact or steal
	•	Good for angling opposition off and slowing down the play
	•	Kicking
	•	Types
	•	Flutter
	•	Dolphin
	•	Efficiency
	•	Down and up stroke of the kick should have equivalent power
	•	Always keep your legs kicking!!!
	•	To follow the play while breathing on the surface
	•	To adjust to the dynamic of the play
	•	To adjust to the “Optimum Positioning”
	•	Swimming the puck and keeping the elbow bent
	•	Provides better leverage when attacking the puck
	•	Helps keep the head up to provide better vision
	•	Maintains a 3D puck control zone for better puck control
	•	Allows better maneuverability to change speed and directions
	•	Shooting/Passing
	•	Straight-Ahead and Across-The-Chest
	•	High Shot and Slider
	•	Inside Flick and Offside Flick
	•	Puck Handling/Dribbling
	•	The Puck Control Zone (The “Yo Zone”)
	•	Puck Handling Patterns
	•	Point-To-Point (A Straight Line)
	•	Triangular
	•	Rectangular/Square
	•	Half-Moon
	•	Full Circle
	•	Ethiopian
	•	Figure 8
	•	Practice! Practice! Practice!         
	•	Practice the above patterns in clockwise and counter clockwise directions
	•	Practice with the puck on the front blade, the back blade and the side of the stick
	•	Curling
	•	Purpose
	•	Protect, maintain and control possession of  the puck
	•	Especially,  when there is no immediate options
	•	Allow the curler to look for alternative options
	•	Allow time for teammate(s) to get into “optimum” positioning to assist
	•	Types
	•	Tight and Wide
	•	High and Low
	•	Slow and Fast
	•	Constant and Variable
	•	Reverse, 180, 270 and Full
	•	Curl’s Control
	•	Controlling the Center of Gravity ➔ Curl’s Control
	•	Lead the curl with the shoulder and pivot on the hip
	•	Do not lead the curl with your head!!!
	•	Keep your head up and feel at the puck
	•	This will provide better vision of the play
	•	Curls are dynamic, vary a curl by:
	•	Varying the speed including stopping/starting
	•	Changing directions
	•	Changing the height of curl
5.   Always Kick Out of Your Curl!!!

4)   The Puck Control Zone
	•	Known as The “Yo” Zone
	•	Fixed spherical area located in middle of the chest
	•	Higher Puck Handling Skills ➔ Larger “Yo” Zone
	•	Protect this 3D zone on offense and
iv.                    Invade this 3D zone on defense
	•	Keep the chest raised to maintain this 3D zone
	•	Just like a Cobra’s defensive position
	•	Laying flat to the bottom collapses the 3D zone ➔ Puck is exposed and is no longer in your control
	•	All moves and tactics are based on positioning the body relative to the "Yo" Zone both offensively and defensively
                           	                                              
5)   Positioning
a.   “Optimal Positioning” =
                               “Yo” Zone Spacing + Body Alignment + Correct Water Depth
	•	Spacing
- Space the body relative to the “Yo” Zone
	•	Body Alignment
                                    - Angle and stretch your body to block the opposition
	•	Receiving a pass IN FRONT of the defender
	•	Place the body between the defender and the receiving zone with you back turn and looking back (over one of the shoulders) at the passer.
	•	Receiving a pass BEHIND of the defender
	•	Be at mid-water in the defender “blind-spot”, making the defender guessing where the receiving zone is at
	•	Be next to defender “Yo” Zone (shoulder-to-shoulder) if the pass falls short, invade and attack the defender Zone
	•	This will take away the defender’s reach-back swat zone with your body
	•	Defending
	•	Head First, Feet First, Sitting and Inverted
	•	Out in the open-pool
	•	Angle the opposition to the outside if in your half court
	•	Angle the opposition to the inside if in their half court
	•	On the side-wall
	•	Avoid stick-to-stick pushing the puck, instead use opposition strength to gain puck control
	•	Make initial stick-to-stick contact to tease
	•	Then, let the opposition push the puck through and into your “Yo” zone
	•	Immediately protect your “Yo” zone by turning your back into the opposition to protect your “Yo” Zone
	•	Along the-end-wall
	•	In a corner
 
	•	Correct Water Depth
	•	Mid Water
	•	Be aware of opponent’s Blind Spot
 
6)   Awareness
	•	Assessment
	•	Know your, your teammates and opposition skill levels
	•	Know what you are looking for
	•	Take a snap-shot of the players most  likely to be involved in the play
	•	Recognition
	•	The team is as strong as the weakest link
	•	Know when things are working or not working
	•	Use opposition strength to gain possession of the puck
	•	Tap the puck to tease the opposition
	•	Then let the opposition push the puck through you
	•	Turn your shoulder quickly to block and get possession of   the puck
	•	Adjustment
	•	Play together as a team and not as an individual
	•	Be on the same page (level of play) to effectively play together as a whole
	•	It is not about you!!!  This is a team’s game
 
7)   Team Work
	•	Offensive Shrinking & Expansion
	•	As a team, know how and when to tighten up the offense
	•	And when to spread/stretch out the defense
	•	Team Skills
	•	Doubling (2-on-1)
	•	Up-and-over (Offensive)
	•	Pretending to surface for a breath
	•	Go up and over behind the defender and back down for the pass
	•	Give-and-go (Offensive)
	•	Quick pass back-and-forth between two players ON ONE BREATH
	•	Pinching/Squeezing (Defensive)
	•	Open-Pool
	•	Squeeze opponent on both sides
	•	Stay on your side and DO NOT follow the curl
	•	On the wall
	•	In the corner
	•	Goal Positioning & Flooding
	•	Defensive
	•	Get down early to gain the real-estate advantage
	•	Outside forwards and midfielders angle bodies pointing away from the goal
	•	Offensive
	•	Take up the Real-Estate
	•	Get down to the bottom BEFORE the opposition and align/stretch your body accordingly to establish your positioning
	•	Sealing the corners
	•	Forwards/strong mid responsibility
	•	At the corner, lean against the end-wall with the strong shoulder
	•	This will not allow defender to use the end-wall
	•	Forcing defender(s) to move the puck from the middle of goal where it is more vulnerable to scoring
	•	Flooding the goal
	•	Inside the 3-meter-area in front of the goal
	•	Seal the corners in case the puck is pop to the edge
	•	Everyone is down except the Full Back/Weak Back to apply more pressure on the defense
	•	Subbing
	•	Purpose
	•	To get the better players into the game
	•	Especially, when your team is behind in point(s)
	•	To get fresh players into the game
	•	Rotate in-and-out quickly
	•	Do not wait to sub between points
	•	Do not wait to sub when you are tired
	•	Play hard and sub quickly
	•	Sub when it is convenient
	•	Especially, when the play is closed to the subbing area
	•	In Water Subbing
	•	Make sure the player’s head who is coming out breaks the surface before submerging your head to get into the game
	•	Out-of-Water Subbing
	•	Maker sure the player’s fins are completely out of the water  before entering the water
	•	Do not dive head first to enter the water, especially when the play is close to the sub area
8)   Strategies
	•	Formations (3-3, 2-3-1. 1-3-2, Wall Game, Arrow Head)
	•	(Side) Wall Usage
	•	It is easier to contain the play along the wall
	•	Focus on the wall zone (up to about 2 meters off the wall)
	•	Try not to keep the puck in actual contact with or very close to the wall
	•	End-Wall Usage
	•	Harder to score on offense from the end-wall
	•	Defender “safe” zone
	•	Corner Usage
	•	On offense
	•	Try to avoid the corner, it is harder to score from the corner, i.e., end-wall
	•	Wait until the defense advance the puck to the 5x2 meter area before attacking
	•	In a desperate situation, to score from the corner, get the puck to the 3 meter zone (or top-of-the-key) and flood the goal
	•	On defense
	•	Defender  “safe” zone
	•	End-Walls & Side Walls
	•	When the puck is in the corner,  especially if the sub area is on the opposite wall
	•	Rest your team for the big push to score
	•	Defender “safe” zone
	•	End-Around
	•	Top of the 3 Meter Circle (Key)
 
9)   Psyche
	•	Visualization
	•	Train your brain to re-act instinctively instead of pro-actively under any situation
	•	Visualize your next move(s) and counter move(s) to beat the opponent
	•	Positive Attitude
	•	Believe in Wining
	•	Believing  in your team and teammates
	•	Mental Toughness
	•	Stay focus and keep pushing even if  the team is behind
 
10)                  Golden Rules
	•	Always keep your head up
	•	Try not to look, but feel the puck on your stick
	•	Keep the chest  raised
	•	➔ Better vision ➔ Better puck control
	•	Rotate the body by leading with the shoulders
	•	Don’t lead with the head
	•	Always keep kicking your legs
	•	Follow the play on the surface by kicking slowly toward the play
	•	Always follow your shots/pass
	•	Because shot/pass may be short
	•	Provide extra support to the receiver and apply more pressure to the opposition
iii.                  Always Kick Out of Your Curls
	•	Positioning is dynamic
	•	Adjust your positioning accordingly to the situation by keep kicking your legs
	•	If the opposition moves,  you must move to get back to the “Optimal Positioning”
	•	Stay down for that extra 1 second
	•	The play or puck may come right back to you
	•	Be patient! Do not follow the curl!!!
	•	Mid-Water positioning
	•	Allow you to use the blind spot to steal from the opposition
	•	Is harder for the opposition to guess your location by not staying on the bottom
 
11)                  Position Responsibilities
	•	Forwards
	•	Puck striker, especially the center forward
	•	Stay in front of the Midfielder and backs
	•	Stay close to opposition “Yo” Zone to receive pass or steal/pick
	•	Receive the puck from passer over your shoulder and NEVER looking directly at the passer
	•	Midfielders
	•	Stay between the backs and the forwards
	•	Support the forwards and help backs defend
	•	Puck distributors
	•	Play offensively in the opposition half court
	•	Play defensively in own half court
	•	Backs
	•	Support forwards and midfielders
	•	Defend the opposition from scoring
	•	Last man standing
 
12)                  Formations
	•	3-3
	•	Forwards play in a triangular pattern
	•	Strong forward is the point of the triangle
	•	Center is the point of the triangle in the middle of the pool
	•	Backs line up in a 45 defensive angle alignment with strong back directly behind the play
	•	2-3-1 (Option 1)
	•	The two forwards cycle their positions
	•	Strong mid and mid-mid cycle their positions in a 45 degree angle
	•	Full back and weak mid cycle their positions
	•	2-3-1 (Option 2)
	•	The two forwards cycle their positions
	•	Strong mid is always behind the strong forward
	•	The mid-mid and the full back cycle their positions
	•	Weak mid is the last man if mid-mid and full back is cycling
	•	Weak mid only engage to cover mid-mid and full back need help
	•	1-3-2 (Option 1)
	•	The forward is the point and the cherry picker
	•	Strong mid and strong back cycle their positions to work with the forward
	•	Mid-mid controls the center of play and backups the strong side of play
	•	Weak mid and weak back cycle their positions
	•	Full back is the last man
	•	1-3-2 (Option 2)
	•	The forward is the cherry picker
	•	Mid-mid and strong mid cycle their positions
	•	Weak mid 45 defensive angle alignment behind the other mids
	•	Weak back and strong back cycle their positions
	•	Corner & Wall Game
	•	Penalty Kick-Out Personnel Adjustment
	•	When a player on a team is kicked out
	•	The team should go into defensive mode and kill the penalized time
	•	By leaving much defenders (midfielders and backs) in the game
	•	Go to a wall or a corner to delay/stall the game
 
13)                  Playing Areas
	•	Green, Gray and Red Zones
	•	Offensive & Defensive Corners
	•	5x2 Meter Zone
	•	3 Meter Zone
	•	In Front of the Goal
 
14)                  Advantage/Disadvantage Puck
	•	Be on the bottom before puck is touched!
	•	Take up the Real-Estate, especially in front of the goal
	•	Forward receiving the puck in the middle of the goal MUST:
	•	Stretch your body fully parallel  to the goal
	•	Back against the defender
	•	Look back toward the passer to receive the puck
	•	Use underhand Ethiopian shove to score
	•	Seal the goal corners
	•	Lean shoulder against the end wall forcing the defender to move the puck toward the middle of their goal which is more vulnerable
	•	3-3:     Outside forwards seal corners
	•	2-3-1:                Forwards seal corners
	•	1-3-2:  The forward and one of the outside mid seal corners
 
15)                  Physical Play & Fouls
	•	Don’t get intimidated
	•	Adjust to the refereeing level and play accordingly
	•	Keep your head in the game, don’t get thrown off by the petty fouls
	•	Quit Whining!!!  You are not the best player in the world!!!!
	•	Without knowing it, you are probably dishing out as may fouls as you are receiving them from the opposition
	•	If you are dishing out the food, then eat some of it!
	•	Shut Up!!!  Don’t be a “Cry Baby”
	•	If the referees are not on the bottom
	•	Lots of calls will be missed, especially under the body infringements.
	•	Use this observation to your advantage.
