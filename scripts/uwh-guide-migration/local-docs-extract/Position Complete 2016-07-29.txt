Swing Offense


	•	Basic role overview
	•	Your job is the control the middle of the formation and the play
	•	Your partners are the wing backs - you must always be ready to support them
	•	You must also be ready to support the forwards and pivot as needed
	•	Surface Backwards unless we are on the attack and you are following the play
	•	When you have the puck
	•	Your first thought should be to pass to the forwards or pivot
	•	If you pass to the pivot, be ready to stay down or repeat to support
	•	Throw the puck as early as safely possible
	•	You can swim straight ahead or towards either side of the formation
	•	If you cannot pass, swim Swim to create a passing option.
	•	Do not curl
	•	All of your help is in front of you All of your help, wing forwards and pivots, are in front of you
	•	If you are in trouble send the puck to the inside forward.
	•	If you are on the wall, send the puck forward and get off the wall.
	•	If you reach the offensive corner, you should not be there. Pass the puck and surface backwards.
	•	You cannot cross the formation (ie stay to the middle) teammates will adjust around you to keep you in the middle of the formation. Delete this bullet
	•	It is acceptable to pass to the wing backs but you must stay down and support until they are in the clear (reset behind you in coverage).
	•	When you no longer have the puck
	•	SURFACE BACKWARDS
	•	When the forward or pivot has the puck behind you
	•	You no longer have the puck, SURFACE BACKWARDS
	•	When the wing backs has the puck behind you
	•	You no longer have the puck, SURFACE BACKWARDS
	•	When the forward on your team has the puck in front of you
	•	You should trail the play in the middle of the formation slightly behind the back that is supporting the play
	•	You should expect the puck to come to you at any time
	•	When the pivot has the puck
	•	You should trail the play in the middle of the formation
	•	An ideal position is 5-6 feet back of the puck
	•	If the pivot charges forward, treat the play like an arrowhead
	•	When we reach the offensive 2 and 5
	•	When the forward or pivot have the puck in front of you
	•	If the backs are not in the play, drop and support be prepared for the puck to drop back to you.
	•	Expect players to center the puck in the formation if they cannot score.
	•	If the puck is centered in front of you go get the puck and go to the goal. 
	•	If the wing backs are in the play there do not over commit, your job is to cover and contain.
	•	When you have the puck
	•	If you have players down in front of you send the puck forward and chase.
	•	If no one is down in front of you go to the goal, help will come
	•	If the puck is in the corner
	•	Expect the puck to be dropped back to either you or a back and we should go to the goal
	•	Do not go into the corner.

Swing Defense

	•	Basic role overview
	•	Control the middle of pool and the opponent's forward line.
	•	Allow the wing forward/backs to control the wall and funnel the puck to you.
	•	No one swims down the middle of the pool without you hitting them.
	•	Force the other team to go lateral into our players. Do not allow them to go vertical.
	•	When the other team has the puck in front of you:
	•	Don't collapse all the way to the wall (you'll be in the way of the wing forward/back).
	•	However, if the wing back get burned, this is acceptable. But don't get in the way of the wing back.
	•	It's expected that both the swing and a wing back will be down at the same time.
	•	You do not have to wait for the wing back to surface to give support.
	•	The wing forwards/backs are going to drive defenders towards you. Be in position to destroy the opposing player.
	•	Trust your wing backs to do their jobs. You do not have to be the first player into every scrum.
	•	Attack from the middle, not from the edges.
	•	If the play comes to you and you cannot control it, force the play laterally rather than give ground.
	•	Attack from the middle unless the puck is in front of our goal.
	•	In front of our goal, the backs attack the puck to the corner. Positions do not matter.
	•	When the other team has the puck behind you:
	•	Whoops; this shouldn't happen
	•	SURFACE BACKWARDS towards the center of the goal
	•	Do not backpick; that is the forward's job


Pivot Offense

	•	Basic role overview
	•	Your job is to control the middle of the formation and the play
	•	You must always be ready to support the wing forwards
	•	You must always be ready to be a forward option for the wing backs and swing
	•	You can and should pass to forwards and backs. 
	•	Do not pass to the swing
	•	If we are on a break, shift slightly forward of parallel to the puck.
	•	Position wise you should be either up with the forwards or 2-3 feet forward of the backs in the middle of the formation.
	•	When you have the puck
	•	Your first thought should be to pass the puck to a teammate in a better position to score. 
	•	If you cannot pass, look to score. 
	•	Do not curl
	•	You can swim straight ahead or towards either side of the formation
	•	You cannot swim outside of the wing forwards because your teammates will adjust to keep you in the middle
	•	If you need help pass to the inside forward or back.
	•	If you end up on the wall, pass the puck to the inside forward, and get off the wall.
	•	If you reach the corner, you should not be there see 2 and 5 rules
	•	You can make moves towards either side of the formation (your teammates will adjust to keep you in the middle)
	•	You can pass to either the wing forwards or the wing backs
	•	Pass should not be long. If you need to throw more than 6 feet, don't make the pass
	•	You cannot leave the puck. You can leave the puck for the back or make a pass to a teammate, except as noted in 3B
	•	When you no longer have the puck
	•	You should surface in the direction the play is going
	•	If you pass to the wing forwards and need to surface, surface forward and chase the play
	•	If you pass to a wing back and need to surface, surface in the direction that the play is going.
	•	If you leave the puck for the wing back on your side and need to surface, surface forward in front of that back
	•	If you lose the puck backwards and need to surface, surface backwards to stay close to the puck
	•	When the backs on your team have the puck behind you
	•	You should be forward of the back (ahead of the puck) within passing distance 
	•	You should be in the middle of the formation
	•	When a back on your team has the puck in front of you
	•	You need to get in front of him. SWIM FORWARD, 
	•	When the swing has the puck
	•	You should be in front of him ready to receive a pass
	•	When we reach the offensive 2 and 5
	•	If you have the puck go to the goal
	•	If you have a teammate ahead of you send the puck forward and chase
	•	If the wing forward has the puck get down and go with him to the goal
	•	If the wing back or swing have the puck
	•	Make yourself and option and be ready to go to the goal
	•	If they are in a battle go get the puck and go to the goal
	•	Do not go to the corner
	•	If the puck is in the corner go get it and drop it to a back or pass the puck to the player closer to the goal. If neither is available wait for team to set up.
	•	If you cannot score drop the puck to the back or swing supporting you on the drive (this is the only time you can leave it for the swing)


Pivot Defense


	•	Basic defensive overview
	•	When the other team has the puck be close behind the play. When the backs knock it free you'll be able to pick it up.
	•	Don't collapse all the way to the wall (you'll be in the way of the wing forward/back).
	•	Your job is to control the middle of pool and the opponent's backfield.
	•	Allow the wing forward/backs to control the wall and funnel the puck to you.
	•	If the other team is on a break, back pick the puck and break up their attack waves
	•	No one swims down the middle of the pool without you hitting them.
	•	When the other team has the puck in front of you:
	•	The wing forwards/backs are going to drive defenders towards you. Be in position to destroy the opposing player.
	•	If the opposing inside back starts a swing and passes to their center back you must challenge the player and not allow him to take pool.
	•	Do not create an opening in the middle of the formation by trying to steal the pass. Stay at home.
	•	When the other team has the puck behind you (closer to the goal we’re defending):
	•	Back pick from the middle. The wings will be attacking from the sides. 
	•	When the puck is in the middle, don't allow the other team to have a person directly behind the person with the puck.
	•	Break up their stack.


Wing Backs Offense

	•	Basic role overview
	•	Surface Backwards
	•	Your job is the control the outside of the formation and the play
	•	Your partner is the wing forward on your side - you must always be ready to support them
	•	When you have the puck
	•	Your first thought should be to pass to your forward or pivot
	•	If you pass to the pivot, be ready to stay down or repeat to support
	•	Throw the puck as early as safely possible
	•	You can swim straight ahead or towards your side of the formation
	•	If you cannot pass, swim
	•	Do not curl with the puck
	•	All of your help is in front of you or towards the middle of the formation
	•	If you start to lose the puck attempt to force it towards the pivot.
	•	If you are on the wall on your side, swim forward up the wall or pass off the wall.
	•	You cannot swim towards the other side of the formation
	•	You can make small moves towards the other side of the formation
	•	You cannot cross the formation
	•	Do not swim the puck into the corner (see 2 and 5 rules)
	•	When you no longer have the puck
	•	SURFACE BACKWARDS
	•	When the forward on your team on your side has the puck behind you
	•	You no longer have the puck, SURFACE BACKWARDS
	•	When the forward on your team on your side has the puck in front of you
	•	You should trail directly behind the forward
	•	Expect the puck to be dropped back to you at any time
	•	When the pivot has the puck
	•	You should be ready to receive a pass on your side of the formation
	•	An ideal passing distance is 5-6 ft away, and 5-6 feet back of the puck
	•	If the pivot charges forward, treat the play like an arrowhead
	•	When the swing has the puck, and you are the inside back
	•	You should be ready to receive a pass on your side of the formation
	•	You should be 5-6 ft away, and 1-2 feet in front of the puck
	•	When the swing has the puck, and you are the outside back
	•	You need to be prepared to support the pass going to the forward in front of you
	•	When the back on your team on the other side of the formation has the puck
	•	If they pass towards the middle of the formation, you should be prepared to receive a pass from the pivot or the swing
	•	 When we reach the offensive 2 and 5
	•	If the forward in front of you has the puck get down and chase the play.
	•	If the pivot has the puck get down and chase the play
	•	If the swing has the puck
	•	If he is moving towards you get down and support
	•	If he is moving away from you drop back and cover
	•	If you have the puck
	•	Go to the goal
	•	If you have a teammate between you and the goal send it forward and chase/close
	•	Do not go to the corner
	•	If the puck is in the corner
	•	Do not go into the corner
	•	Expect the forward or the pivot to drop it back to you
	•	Go to the goal or pass to a player closer to the goal.


Wing Backs Defense


	•	Basic role overview
	•	Surface Backwards
	•	Your job is the control the outside of the formation and the play
	•	The other team cannot beat you wide. You are the outside contain.
	•	Your partner is the wing forward on your side - you must always be ready to support them
	•	When the other team has the puck in front of you:
	•	Do not let the play go down our wall
	•	Funnel their forwards to the pivot
	•	Once the puck is lost in front of you, you should chase the play from your side of the formation.
	•	If you cannot control the play, force the play to pivot or wall and surface backwards quickly.
	•	If you are the open-side back, and the swing forces the play to your side, attack the play aggressively.
	•	You are now the inside back.
	•	Attack from the outside unless the puck is in front of our goal.
	•	In front of our goal, the backs attack the puck to the corner. Positions do not matter.
	•	If you get the puck in front of the goal while defending, look for opportunities to attack to the open side.
	•	When the other team has the puck behind you:
	•	Whoops; this shouldn't happen
	•	Surface backwards towards the center of the goal
	•	Get back into position to make a good stop on the wall (give up ground if you have to)
	•	Do not backpick; that is the forward's job

Wing Forwards Offense


	•	Basic role overview
	•	Your job is the control the outside of the formation and the play
	•	Your partner is the wing back on your side - you must always be ready to support them
	•	You should always look to score first pass second
	•	When you have the puck
	•	Your first thought should be to score
	•	If you cannot score, draw in two defenders to defend you
	•	Do not curl
	•	You can swim straight ahead or towards your side of the formation
	•	All of your help is behind you or towards the middle of the formation
	•	If you lose the puck, lose it backwards towards the wall or force it to the pivot.
	•	If you are on the wall on your side, swim forward up the wall or pass to the pivot.
	•	You cannot swim towards the other side of the formation
	•	You can make small moves towards the other side of the formation
	•	You cannot cross the formation
	•	Your only passing option is the pivot
	•	This should not be a long pass
	•	If you need to throw more than 6 feet, don't make the pass
	•	You can lay the puck back to the wing back on your side.

	•	When you no longer have the puck
	•	You should surface in the direction the play is going
	•	If you pass to the pivot, surface to a spot forward of the pivot, on your side of the formation
	•	If you leave the puck for the wing back on your side, surface forward in front of that back
	•	If you lose the puck backwards, surface backwards to stay close to the puck
	•	When the back on your team on your side has the puck behind you
	•	You should be 2-3 ft wider to your side than the back
	•	If the back is on your wall side, you should be on the wall directly in front of the back
	•	When the back on your team on your side has the puck in front of you
	•	You should move to get back in front of him
	•	If the back is on a sprint or you are coming in from a sub you can drop in behind him briefly.
	•	When the pivot has the puck
	•	You should be ready to receive a pass on your side of the formation
	•	An ideal passing distance is 5-6 ft away, and 1-2 feet forward of the puck
	•	If the pivot charges forward, treat the play like a arrowhead
	•	When the swing has the puck
	•	You should be ready to receive a pass on your side of the formation
	•	When the back on your team on the other side of the formation has the puck
	•	If they pass towards the pivot or the swing, you should be prepared to receive a pass from the pivot or the swing
	•	When we reach the offensive 2 and 5
	•	Inside Forward
	•	If you have the puck turn and go to the goal
	•	Do not go to the corner
	•	If you end up in the corner you should not be there
	•	Either center the puck to a teammate 
	•	Or drop it back (along the side wall and away from the corner)  to the back on your side
	•	If you have a teammate between you and the goal pass it forward and case.
	•	Openside Forward
	•	If you have the puck go the goal
	•	If you cannot get to the goal
	•	Center to a teammate with a better chance of scoring
	•	Drop it to the back on your side
	•	Do not go to the corner
	•	If the pivot, swing, or back has the puck get down and go with them to the goal.


Wing Forwards Defense



	•	Basic role overview
	•	Your job is the control the outside of the formation and the play
	•	Your partner is the wing back on your side - you must always be ready to support them
	•	The other team cannot beat you wide. You are the outside contain.
	•	When the other team has the puck in front of you:
	•	Do not let the play go down our wall
	•	Once the puck is lost in front of you, you should chase the play from your side of the formation.
	•	If you cannot get the puck, force the play to pivot or to the back on your side (contain)
	•	Funnel their forwards to the pivot
	•	If you are the open side forward AND the puck has just been passed from the opposing center back to open side back
	•	Challenge the player and force him to the pivot or the wall. Do not let him get a pass off.
	•	When the other team has the puck behind you:
	•	Don’t allow back passes. Get between their players so they can't stack up.
	•	Break up their stack
	•	Backpick
	•	Pick the puck from your side of the formation
	•	If you pick the puck, move to your side of the formation to get going on offense
	•	If puck is on the wall, get between the other team and the wall and force it off the wall to the pivot.
