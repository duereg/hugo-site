Underwater hockey is a fun, fast, three-dimensional game played on the bottom of a pool. Two teams move a puck along the floor and score by putting it in the opponent’s goal with a short stick.

Gear: fins, mask, snorkel, protective glove, water-polo cap. You drop to the bottom of the pool on short breath holds, then surface to recover.

## Your first practice

You do not need to be a swimmer. Many people (including myself) did not come from any formal swim background.

Typical session: gear fit, surface warm-up with the snorkel, short dives down and back, first puck contact, then a small drill or beginner game.

Day one: feel safe, repeat short drops, sit briefly on the bottom and surface calmly, move the puck with a stick.

Out of breath? Surface early. Short drops beat heroic ones.

## What to bring

Clubs usually loan sticks, masks, snorkels, gloves, and caps at first. Bring what you can:

- Your own mask (if you own one)
- Swimsuit
- Sunscreen (if an outdoor pool)
- Towel

## Common beginner mistakes

- **Frantic breathing before a dive.** Slow inhale, calm exhale.
- **Staying down too long.** Surface early; stack short drops.
- **Chasing a puck wildly.** Approach under control; stop it with the stick.
- **Staring at the puck.** Touch, then look up.
- **Leaking mask or bad snorkel fit.** Fix gear first.
- **Hands on the puck.** Stick only.
- **Two Hands on the Stick.** Sticks are one handed, two hands is a foul
