## Stick and puck (solo or group)

**Star under the torso.** Trace a star shape with the puck under your chest. One line at a time, then connect sides, then both directions. Head up when you can. [Atlantis — Star Drill](https://wiki.atlantissports.org/players/individual_drills/star-drill).

**Passing circle.** Players in a ring; flick around. Variants: touch moves before the pass; curl then flick; two pucks opposite ways. Wrist warm-up before games. [Atlantis — Passing Circles](https://wiki.atlantissports.org/teams/teamwork_drills/passing-circle).

**Diamond (once you have curls / dekes).** Mark four points on the bottom. Swim a set path: deke, figure-6 or deep-V, curl, pass back. [Setup #1](https://wiki.atlantissports.org/players/individual_drills/diamond_drill_1).

## Two and three players

**2-on-1 finish.** One defender, two attackers. Seal the DSZ; release close to the stick. [Two-on-One]({{< ref "12-two-on-one.md" >}}).

**Pinch pairs.** One puck carrier, two chasers. Both close on opposite sides; win the puck without screening. [Pinches]({{< ref "07a-pinches.md" >}}).

**Braid (curl + back pass).** Three players in a lane-width channel. Swim diagonal, curl outside, pass straight back to the trailer; rotate. [Atlantis — Braid](https://wiki.atlantissports.org/teams/teamwork_drills/braid).

**Swing across.** Stack "defense" on one wall; offense deep- or lateral-swings to the open side, then advances. Keep a recovery body on deep swings.

## Full-court scraps

**3-3 half-pool.** Live play; whistle major gaps between forwards and backs. [Cycling]({{< ref "11-cycling-breakaways.md" >}}).

**Advantage restart reps.** Set [advantage / equal]({{< ref "08a-restarts.md" >}}) and run the first three touches until spacing is automatic.

Keep sets short. Surface when you need air ([Safety]({{< ref "00a-safety.md" >}})).
