You play the left or right outside back.

In the Seven formation, the **strong-side back** (puck side) is usually on the bottom. Same job either way: hold your side and don't get beat to the outside.

Your partner is the wing forward on your side.

For how the backs line up and cycle, see [Backs in the 3-3]({{< ref "07b-backs-in-the-3-3.md" >}}).

## Offense

### With the puck

- Pass early to your wing forward or the [center forward]({{< ref "center-forward.md" >}}).
- Swim straight or toward your side. Don't cross the formation.
- Help is in front of you or toward the middle.
- Losing the puck? Force it to the center forward.
- On your wall: swim up the wall or pass off the wall.
- Don't pass to the [swing back]({{< ref "swing-back.md" >}}).
- Puck in your wall channel: play tight to the wall. If it comes back from the forwards, pass up to the forward, carry up the wall, or hit the center forward at 45°. If you pass to the center forward, chase until the play clears to the other wing.

### Without the puck

- Surface backwards.

### Supporting a teammate

- Wing forward behind you: surface backwards.
- Wing forward in front of you: trail behind them. Expect a drop-back.
- Center forward with the puck: ready on your side, 5–6 feet away and 5–6 feet back.
- Swing with the puck, and you're inside: ready 5–6 feet away, 1–2 feet in front.
- Swing with the puck, and you're openside: support the pass to the forward in front of you.

## Defense

- Hold the outside. Don't get beat wide.
- Don't let play go down your wall.
- Funnel opposing forwards to the center forward.
- Attack from outside in — unless the puck is right in front of your goal (then all backs go to the corner; positions don't matter).
- Puck lost in front of you: chase from your side.
- Can't control it? Force to the center forward or the wall, then surface backwards.
- Openside back when the swing forces play your way: attack hard — you're the inside back now.

## Quick reference

- Surface backwards when you're not on the puck.
- Pass early to forward or center forward. Don't pass to the swing.
- Stay on your side. Don't swim lateral off the wall.
- Contain the outside. Play tight in your wall channel.
